Texture orientation - faces from the texture scan review

These faces had their texture turned or mirrored so it did not run on from the faces next to it.
Each was found by ALTLevelScan, rendered before and after, and kept after review (Edward, 2026).

A turn rotates the face's four vertex indices (16 bytes at the face's offset), so the face keeps
its winding. A mirror swaps the flag byte (offset + 0x12) for its mirrored partner: 0 and 2,
4 and 6, 12 and 34, 8 and 10, 26 and 28, 30 and 32. The texture index and light bytes are
unchanged.
