Texture orientation - faces from the texture scan review

These faces had their texture turned or mirrored so it did not run on from the faces next to it.
Each was found by ALTLevelScan, rendered before and after, and kept after review (Edward, 2026).

A turn rotates the face's four vertex indices (16 bytes at the face's offset), so the face keeps
its winding. A mirror swaps the flag byte (offset + 0x12) for its mirrored partner: 0 and 2,
4 and 6, 12 and 34, 8 and 10, 26 and 28, 30 and 32. The texture index and light bytes are
unchanged.

The start room's way out (Claude, 2026)

The player starts in a small room at (1,38) whose doorway (the attribute 6 cells at x = 4) leads to
the corridor. The four cells just past it, (5,37) to (5,40), have attribute 1, which the level load
makes a solid wall (0x282f5 sets +7 to 0x80), though nothing is drawn there: the corridor is open to
look at and impossible to enter, so a game started on this map could not leave the room. The same
room on L391 has attribute 0 there. Each cell's attribute byte (+0x0a of its 16-byte record) is set
to 0, open floor; the floor and ceiling heights are unchanged.
