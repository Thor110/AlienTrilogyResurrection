Texture orientation - faces from the texture scan review

These faces had their texture turned or mirrored so it did not run on from the faces next to it.
Each was found by ALTLevelScan, rendered before and after, and kept after review (Edward, 2026).

A turn rotates the face's four vertex indices (16 bytes at the face's offset), so the face keeps
its winding. A mirror swaps the flag byte (offset + 0x12) for its mirrored partner: 0 and 2,
4 and 6, 12 and 34, 8 and 10, 26 and 28, 30 and 32. The texture index and light bytes are
unchanged.

The start room stays sealed (Claude, 2026)

The player's header start is in a small room at (1,38) whose doorway (the attribute 6 cells at x = 4) leads to
the corridor. The four cells just past it, (5,37) to (5,40), have attribute 1, which the level load makes a
solid wall (0x282f5 sets +7 to 0x80), though nothing is drawn there. An earlier patch set them to 0 so the
level could be looked round from the header's start; that is undone. The room is the multiplayer players'
holding room, sealed on purpose: its lift carries developer graffiti ("RIPLEY WOZ ERE" and the like) in
colourful textures that match nothing else in the level (Edward, 2026), and the original starts a
multiplayer player at a random path node, skipping node 0 on this level. ALTEngine now does the same in
Level Select on a multiplayer map. Each cell's attribute byte (+0x0a of its 16-byte record) is set back to
01; on a file as shipped this is already so, and only a copy the earlier patch changed is altered.

The second holding room (Claude, 2026)

The same room, mirrored, is at the corridor's east end (82-84, 37-40), behind a lift door painted on the
corridor's wall (faces with textures 0-5 at x = 17408) and a real door (door 0, at (81,38), unlocked). The
four cells in front of the painted door, (80,37) to (80,40), have attribute 0 where the start room's have 1,
so a player could walk through the painted door, open the real one and walk in. They are set to 1, a solid
wall at load, as at the other end; nothing drawn changes. Neither room is used: both are taken out by the
Level Cleanup (data/LevelCleanupExtra.json, "rooms").
