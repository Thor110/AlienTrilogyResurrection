L111LEV.MAP and L900LEV.MAP incorrect texture index
0x46DC4 = 38

The face is the triangle 8052, 8049, 8113 at 0x46DB4. With the right texture index its texture also
needs a quarter-turn counter-clockwise. That used to be done in the engine at render time; it is now
in the file, by rotating the triangle's vertex list one place, as the texture scan fixes do:

Offset		Was				Now
0x46DB4		8052, 8049, 8113, -1	->	8049, 8113, 8052, -1
		74 1F 00 00 71 1F 00 00 B1 1F 00 00 FF FF FF FF
		->	71 1F 00 00 B1 1F 00 00 74 1F 00 00 FF FF FF FF
0x46DC4		F1			->	38

The rotation keeps the face's winding and its texture index, flag and light bytes. Checked against the
engine's old render-time rotation: the rebuilt level mesh is identical, vertex for vertex.

The same bug at the same offsets in both levels; L900LEV previously got neither fix.
Applied by ALTEngine (data/Patches.json) and by ALTViewer's patch button.
