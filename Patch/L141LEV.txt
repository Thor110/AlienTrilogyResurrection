Fixes moved from ALTViewer's model export into the file (Claude, 2026)

ALTViewer's OBJ export used to correct Weyland-Yutani and grey crate faces by giving them other UVs, so the fix never reached
the game. Each one is now a byte change that gives the same result in the level file itself.

A turn rotates the face's four vertex indices (16 bytes at the face's offset), keeping its winding. A mirror
sets the flag byte (offset + 0x12) to its mirrored partner. A texture change sets the texture index
(offset + 0x10, two bytes) to the one the export borrowed. The old export remap is listed so the mapping
stays on record: "face N's UVs as a,b,c,d" means corner k took corner p[k] of face N's UVs.

The end room's floor (Edward, FaceLog, 2026; Claude, 2026)

The floor of the room at the level's end, cells (27-30, 0-4) (x -6914 to -4866, z -26880 to -24320), is
four tiles across and five deep. Its strips are one picture, 128 x 32, cut into four 32-texel slices: an end,
two different middles and the other end. L111's spawn floor uses all four in order, unturned (textures 299,
300, 301, 302), and runs on seamlessly. L141's sheet held only the first two - 221 (L111's 299) and 222
(L111's 300) - so the room repeated one middle and turned the ends about, and the weave broke at every join;
no turning or mirroring of 221 and 222 can make it whole (a repeated middle leaves a seam, a mirrored one a V).

141GFX.B16 (and 903GFX.B16, whose page 3 is the same byte for byte): the two missing slices go into texture
slots no face, animated texture or door or lift model of L141 or L903 uses - 223 (page 3 at 224,96, right
after 222) for the second middle and 246 (page 3 at 192,192) for the far end. Each texel is one of L141's own
colour indices: for each texel of L111's 301 and 302, the place in L111's 299/300 whose 5 x 5 surroundings
match it best (same row, or one off) gives the L141 texel at that place in 221/222. So the slices keep L141's
darker colours (Edward) - their average colour is within half a step of 221's and 222's - and every join
steps no more than the texture does inside a tile. Page 3's pixels start at 0x30B0C (TP03 + 8); 32 rows of
32 bytes each, at 0x30B0C + (y + row) * 256 + x.

L141LEV.MAP (and L903LEV.MAP, made from L141, the same faces at the same offsets): each of the first four rows
is now 221, 222, 223, 246 across the room, each tile's own four corners turned (a rotation, so it still faces
up) to run as row 1's do; routine 0 throughout. The fifth row is texture 73.
