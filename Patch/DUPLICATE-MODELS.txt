Duplicate and unused door/lift models - removed (Claude, 2026)

A level's door models are its "D" chunks and its lift models its "L" chunks, after MAP0. The game keeps
a pointer to each in a table, in file order, and a door or lift draws table[its record's model number] at
its own position, so one model can serve any number of doors (the shipped levels already share them).
These chunks were byte-identical to an earlier one, or no record pointed at them. Each removal drops the
chunks, rewrites the FORM size at offset 4 (file length - 8), and renumbers the door records (byte 7) and
lift records (byte 12) listed. The tags of the chunks that stay are not renamed; only their first letter is read.

It is applied before every other patch, which is written against the shortened files. A removal is made
only if each chunk is there with the size and CRC-32 below, and does nothing if it has already been made.
L162LEV-D001's texture fix moves with it, from 0x323E8 to 0x3205C. Full list and reasoning:
Notes/duplicate-level-models.md.
