Texture orientation - faces from the texture scan review

These faces had their texture turned or mirrored so it did not run on from the faces next to it.
Each was found by ALTLevelScan, rendered before and after, and kept after review (Edward, 2026).

A turn rotates the face's four vertex indices (16 bytes at the face's offset), so the face keeps
its winding. A mirror swaps the flag byte (offset + 0x12) for its mirrored partner: 0 and 2,
4 and 6, 12 and 34, 8 and 10, 26 and 28, 30 and 32. The texture index and light bytes are
unchanged.

==== ALTENGINE ONLY - NOT FOR THE ORIGINAL GAME (stage 5, ALTEngine/data/EngineFilePatches.json, "modelFaces") ====

The lift platform (model L000, used by all 13 of the level's 4 x 4 lifts). Its two corner triangles at the
high-X/high-Z and low-X/low-Z corners, faces 1 and 14, show tile 297 cut the wrong way, so the vein from each of the
four eyes stops at them. Each becomes the mirror image of its neighbour across the lift: face 7 onto face 1 and
face 8 onto face 14 (Edward, 2026).

Why ALTEngine only: a mirrored triangle needs its corners reversed, which turns it to face down. The original's
lift loader (FUN_00041a20) only ever gives a lift face draw routine 0, 1 or 2, and all three hide a face seen from
behind, so in the original these two corners would be gaps. ALTEngine marks the faces (their +0x13 byte, which the
original never reads from the file: its loader writes the routine number there) and draws a marked door or lift face
from both sides. ALTViewer's patch button does not make this edit, and the original game should never be given it.

The edit names the face by section and number, not by offset, so it holds with the Level Cleanup on or off. It is
made after the Level Cleanup and the texture blanking, and taken out again at the start of every install run.

Corners (7, 4, 3, 3) -> (4, 7, 3, 3) and (19, 2, 20, 20) -> (2, 19, 20, 20); texture 297 and flag 2 unchanged.
In the shipped file (after the duplicate model removals) the records are at 0x7D10C and 0x7D210; the install finds
them by section, since the Level Cleanup moves them.
