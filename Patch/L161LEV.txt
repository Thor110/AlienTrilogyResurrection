Texture orientation - faces from the texture scan review

These faces had their texture turned or mirrored so it did not run on from the faces next to it.
Each was found by ALTLevelScan, rendered before and after, and kept after review (Edward, 2026).

A turn rotates the face's four vertex indices (16 bytes at the face's offset), so the face keeps
its winding. A mirror swaps the flag byte (offset + 0x12) for its mirrored partner: 0 and 2,
4 and 6, 12 and 34, 8 and 10, 26 and 28, 30 and 32. The texture index and light bytes are
unchanged.

Fixes moved from ALTViewer's model export into the file (Claude, 2026)

ALTViewer's OBJ export used to correct the secretion-covered wall by giving it other UVs, so the fix never reached
the game. Each one is now a byte change that gives the same result in the level file itself.

A turn rotates the face's four vertex indices (16 bytes at the face's offset), keeping its winding. A mirror
sets the flag byte (offset + 0x12) to its mirrored partner. A texture change sets the texture index
(offset + 0x10, two bytes) to the one the export borrowed. The old export remap is listed so the mapping
stays on record: "face N's UVs as a,b,c,d" means corner k took corner p[k] of face N's UVs.
