Walk-through pillars (Claude, 2026)

L903LEV is built on L141LEV: the same 105 x 81 grid and the same geometry. Four of its cells lost their
attribute byte (cell offset + 10) on the way, so the two pillars at (11,43) and (11,48) could be walked
into. Restored to L141's values; attributes 0x14-0x2c block.

0x69502		(11,43)		00	14	pillar
0x6AE52		(11,48)		00	14	pillar
0x6E862		(49,59)		00	15
