FINGERS.B16 - ALTENGINE ONLY (stage 5). Never applied to the original game; ALTViewer's patch button leaves it alone.

The face hugger crawling onto the camera is two halves of one mirrored picture, four frames each (the file's two
sections, F001 the left half and F000 the right; animations 16 and 17 of the hugger's own tables). Once it has
arrived, every frame ended its drawn art two sprite rows short of the bottom of the view - four rows of the
320x240 view at the double size it is drawn at - so a strip of the world showed under the creature (Edward,
2026). The frames run on below the art in one to three empty rows; the original's 200-row screen hides the gap.

The fix: under each frame's art, two rows that copy its last two rows, in order (one row repeated looks
stretched - Edward). Five frames had room inside them. Three had one empty row and are one row taller:

  half A (F001)  frame 0  60 x 92        frame 1  52 x 88 -> 89   frame 2  56 x 88        frame 3  64 x 88
  half B (F000)  frame 0  60 x 88        frame 1  64 x 88         frame 2  56 x 88 -> 89  frame 3  52 x 92 -> 93

A frame's size is in the executable's tables, not the file, so ALTEngine reads those three heights from
Screens/FaceHugOverlay.h (FIXED_HEIGHTS); with the file as shipped it still loads the shipped heights.

The frames are compressed (the game's LZSS). Each one still fits in the space it had, so no frame moves, the
tables' offsets hold and the file's size (17152 bytes) is unchanged. Five frames keep their own compressed
bytes up to the last row of art and end in one copy of the two rows above; three (half A frame 2, half B
frames 1 and 3) are compressed again whole, which comes out smaller than the shipped bytes. The pictures are
exactly what ALTEngine made at load before (FaceHugOverlay::RepeatBottomRows, zips 154-168, now removed).

Checked by CRC-32: 0x0A5E789D as shipped -> 0x9B6FE6D7 fixed (ALTEngine/data/EngineFilePatches.json).
