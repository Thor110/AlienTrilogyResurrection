^ Inaccessible Secret Fix (discovered by @bambamalicious)

0x6F78E is byte 10 of lift 3's record: its threshold, the count its triggers must reach
before it moves (FUN_000423e8 copies it to the lift's +0x18; the lift's idle task waits
for counter >= threshold). The original value was 2.

Lift 3 is the 3x3 lift at cells (21-23, 58-60). It starts at the top (flags 0x01), at
height 48, and goes down 48 into secret area 1, the pit at height 0. Two triggers add
to it, and nothing else in the level refers to it:

Trigger 5	cells (21,59) (22,59) (22,60), on the lift	unlimited uses
	command 4: op 5, add 1 to lift 3 - only as the FINAL count (FUN_00041e20
	refuses unless counter >= threshold - 1)
Trigger 6	cells (16,59) (17,59), in front of switch object 0 (type 0x1B, large switch,
	at (16,58), facing south)	unlimited uses, needs the use key
	command 5: op 4, throw switch object 0 (FUN_00036f4c; type 0x1B needs no battery)
	command 6: op 7, add 1 to lift 3 (FUN_00041dd8)

The switch and trigger 6 are at the bottom of the pit, inside the secret area. So with a
threshold above 1, stepping on the lift at the top is refused (the counter is 0, not
threshold - 1), and the switch that would add the other count can only be reached on the
lift: the secret can never be entered. With 1, stepping on takes the lift down; it has
no dwell, so at the bottom it clears its counter and waits, and stepping on it again (or
the switch) brings it back up. That is what the two triggers are for, so 1 is the value
the level was built for, not a workaround.
